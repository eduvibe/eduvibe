### 👋 About Me

> Building technology that actually works in real classrooms and school offices.

I'm **Okolo Uchenna Maxwell**, a **Software Developer, EdTech Builder, and Computer Science Educator** based in Nigeria. Founder & CEO of [EduMax Solutions](https://www.edumaxsolutions.com.ng/) — with **6+ years** of hands-on experience in teaching and school management, I bridge the gap between traditional education and modern technology.

I don't just write code — I solve real problems I’ve seen firsthand in schools: unreliable internet, exam malpractice, manual result processing, parent communication gaps, and cash-based student spending.

**Mission:** To deliver impactful, user-friendly, and **offline-first** software that enhances learning outcomes and streamlines school operations across Nigeria and beyond.
